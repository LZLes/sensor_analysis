    Paste a layout from Excel

    An 8×12 block of labels, laid out like the plate. Blank marks blanks, Std 10 marks a standard at 10, and anything else is a sample split into subject and timepoint (e.g. P01_D7). Empty cells are skipped.

    Import plate data in ① first.

    A plate without its own standards can use another plate's curve; its own blank is used if it has one.

    Standards

    Recovery = back-calculated ÷ nominal concentration; ⚠ marks levels outside 80–120%. To leave out an outlier replicate, use Exclude on ② Plate Layout.

    Per sample

    Mean ± SD of the replicate wells' back-calculated concentrations. Wells that can't be back-calculated or are excluded are left out of the mean; flags show replicates outside the standard range or below the LOD/LOQ.

    Per well

    Highlighted rows are flagged. Untick Use to exclude a well (from this plate's means and, for standards, its curve).

    Results plate map

    Sample wells show back-calculated concentration.

    Normalise by area

    Per sample: sample conc = well conc × dilution, amount = sample conc × volume (when a volume is given), per area = amount (or sample conc) ÷ area. Leave dilution blank for 1. Pasting a column from Excel into a cell fills the cells below it.
